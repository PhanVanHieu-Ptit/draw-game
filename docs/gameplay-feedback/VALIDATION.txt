Arcane Paws — Gameplay feedback validation

Delivered
- Numeric HP alongside hearts, critical-health styling, temporary protection time and a stable shield ring.
- Persistent combo count and score multiplier, remaining combo time, an explicit expiration warning and a separate combo-break state.
- Remaining hostile wave count, fixed boss phase/life/rune progress, and per-spell ready/cooldown states when cooldowns are enabled.
- A threat cue selects the enemy with the shortest estimated time to collision and displays its direction and next required rune. Hidden runes remain hidden.
- Enemy types have distinct glyphs and names. The active rune has a pointer, larger border and stronger emphasis. Secondary badges collapse during crowded battles.
- Cast success, Perfect Cast, multiple targets, incorrect/ambiguous input, secret spells, blocked hits and unavailable cooldowns have explicit messages.
- Independent cast and announcement surfaces prevent routine results from replacing boss transitions. Repeated messages do not extend themselves; stale notifications are discarded.
- Routine dialogue/toasts yield to imminent danger. One general toast is visible at a time. Particles render behind enemies and their rune badges.
- Persisted optional compact HUD in Settings > Graphics. Short landscape screens automatically prioritize health, score, combo, controls and threat information.
- Three full resume countdown seconds with input disabled. HP, invulnerability, combo and cooldown time stay frozen through pause and countdown. The pause button remains available during the countdown.

Game feel and accessibility
- Ordinary cast shake is 1.8 pixels / 100 ms; major camera shake is capped at 8 pixels / 250 ms. Existing settings can disable it.
- Crowded encounters halve hit/death particle density. Reduced effects use a quarter-density particle budget while retaining visible impacts.
- Reduced effects disable feedback CSS animations, camera shake and lightning flashes. Shield and warning cues remain steady and readable. OS reduced-motion defaults remain supported.
- Health uses a live status with numeric HP. Boss progress exposes current lives and rune count. Threat announcements change with the cue, rather than announcing a timer every frame.
- Touch controls retain 44-pixel minimum targets. Keyboard alternatives and 1–8 rune input remain available.
- Input trails remain in logical screen coordinates, independent of camera shake. Projectile travel, impact timing and the existing enemy hit reaction duration are preserved.
- Distinct cast, impact and error audio remains. Combo expiration no longer repeats a combo chime; Perfect/chain notifications no longer add a second impact sound at launch. Boss transition audio remains.
- Scoring, damage amounts, combo tiers, projectile damage resolution and feature flags were not changed. Perfect Cast and cooldown presentation reflect the configured rules; these features are not enabled by this change.

Limits
Chrome viewport emulation was used, not physical phones. No manual screen-reader or audio listening evaluation was performed. Crowded screenshots deliberately park enemies close together; this work limits feedback overlap without changing enemy spawn, movement, collision, damage or scoring mechanics. This is functional/layout validation, not a comparative frame-rate benchmark.
